## The five dimensions

1. Discoverable: can it find your catalog? Sitemap, robots, links that do not depend on JavaScript.
2. Readable: does it understand what you sell? Product, price, currency and availability in data a machine reads.
3. Actionable: can it do more than look? Declared interfaces: OpenAPI, MCP, A2A, feeds.
4. Transactable: can it get all the way to the purchase? Shipping, returns and payment with rules it can read.
5. Governed: did you tell agents what they may do? Agent policy, contact and security.
