The method

Five questions an agent asks before buying from you.

01

Discoverable

Can it find your catalog? Sitemap, robots, links that do not depend on JavaScript.

02

Readable

Does it understand what you sell? Product, price, currency and availability in data a machine reads.

03

Actionable

Can it do more than look? Declared interfaces: OpenAPI, MCP, A2A, feeds.

04

Transactable

Can it get all the way to the purchase? Shipping, returns and payment with rules it can read.

05

Governed

Did you tell agents what they may do? Agent policy, contact and security.
